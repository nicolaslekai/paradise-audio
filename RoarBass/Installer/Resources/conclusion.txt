RoarBass is installed.

Launch RoarBass once from /Applications so macOS registers the Audio Unit,
then open Logic Pro and pick AU Instruments ▸ Anthropic ▸ RoarBass on any
instrument track.

If the plug-in doesn't appear, open Terminal and run:

    auval -v aumu Roar Anth

That will print Logic's validation log and tell you what (if anything)
needs attention.
