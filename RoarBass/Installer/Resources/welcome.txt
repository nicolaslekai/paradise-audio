Welcome to the RoarBass installer.

RoarBass is an AUv3 instrument plug-in for Logic Pro, GarageBand, MainStage,
and other Audio Unit hosts on macOS.

This installer will place RoarBass.app into /Applications. The Audio Unit
itself lives inside the app bundle (that's how AUv3 ships on macOS) — once
the app has been launched at least once, the AU is registered system-wide.

After install, open Logic Pro and add an instrument track. RoarBass will
appear under AU Instruments ▸ Anthropic ▸ RoarBass.
